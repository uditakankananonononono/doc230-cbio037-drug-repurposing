# AUDIT PREREG - 037 (written before the audit ran)
Concern: in the cold-compound setting, positives come from compounds that have known indications (well-annotated drugs), while negatives are uniform random compounds, most of which are poorly annotated. A disease-independent "how annotated is this compound" score could separate them without any disease-specific signal. The original "degree-matched" negatives matched only on CtD degree (+1), which is constant for held-out compounds, so it did not control compound annotation density. The D10 B1 baseline is also degenerate: held-out and non-CtD candidate compounds all have train CtD degree 0, so B1 ranks them by a random tie-break.
Data: Hetionet v1.0 re-fetched, SHA256 re-verified. Same features, models, seeds (0-4), splits (cold-compound) as src/run.py (executed from it).
Q1 B4 = compound gene-degree (number of distinct gene neighbours via CbG/CuG/CdG; disease-independent), AUROC in the original D2 setting (random negatives, cold-compound). Pre-specified: if B4 >= M - 0.02, M's cold-compound signal is "compound annotation density, not disease-specific".
Q2 Compound-matched negatives: negatives use the SAME compound pool as the positives of that split (train positives' compounds for training; held-out positives' compounds for test) paired with random diseases that are not known CtD/CpD for that compound. Models M, B2, B3, B4 trained/evaluated under this regime (cold-compound), 5 seeds. Pre-specified: M AUROC > 0.60 means a disease-specific signal exists; <= 0.60 means it does not.
Q3 D10 hits@10 re-run with B4 and a truly random ranking expectation (mean over 20 random draws) alongside M and B3; B1 reported as degenerate. Pre-specified: if B4 hits@10 >= M hits@10 - 0.05, D10 does not show disease-specific ranking.
Original gates are not re-fit; changes to verdicts are written to README/RESULTS.
