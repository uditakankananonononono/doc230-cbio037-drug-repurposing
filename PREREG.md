# CBIO037 "A Novel Deep Learning Method for Drug Repurposing" - preregistration (written before any model is scored)
Status: replication-style benchmark on public data. No novelty claim. Written and committed before results.
Data feasibility: Hetionet v1.0 (public, https://github.com/hetio/hetionet), CPU only. Target: Compound-treats-Disease (CtD, 755 edges). Also CpD (palliates, 390) in D8.
Features (no CtD/CpD edges used as features): compound profile = binary vector over genes from CbG/CuG/CdG (binds/up/down-regulates); disease profile = genes from DaG/DuG/DdG. Both reduced by TruncatedSVD (64 d, fit on all nodes, unsupervised, no labels). Compound side-effect (CcSE) profile also reduced (32 d).
Positives: CtD edges. Negatives: uniformly random compound-disease pairs that are not CtD/CpD, 1:1 (D6: degree-matched).
Models: B1 degree product (train CtD degree of compound x disease, +1 smoothing; needs train labels). B2 logistic regression on elementwise product + concatenation of SVD profiles. B3 gene-overlap score (cosine of compound gene profile with disease gene profile, no training). M: MLP (2 hidden layers of 128, dropout 0.3, Adam, 30 epochs) on concatenated profiles + product. 
Splits: random edge split (80/20), cold-compound (20% of compounds with CtD held out entirely), cold-disease (20% of diseases held out). 5 seeds; report mean and sd. Metrics: AUROC, AUPRC on test positives + matched negatives.
Gates: D2 M cold-compound AUROC >= best baseline + 0.02. D3 random-split minus cold-compound AUROC for M <= 0.05 (no leakage-driven inflation).
Directions: D1 data stats; D2 cold-compound comparison; D3 random-vs-cold gap; D4 cold-disease; D5 label-shuffle negative control (M trained on shuffled labels should give AUROC ~0.5); D6 degree-matched negatives; D7 feature ablation (gene profiles only vs side-effect only vs both); D8 CpD (palliates) as target, cold-compound; D9 seed variability; D10 hits@10 per held-out disease (cold-compound, fraction of diseases where a true treating compound is in top 10 of all compounds), versus B1/B3. Top-ranked predictions are NOT validated claims.
Not run: GNN / metapath-DWPC methods (CPU/time); stated.
